Test your understanding of R data types, vectors, lists, data frames, functions and basic programming. Each attempt contains 10 questions selected from a bank of 100.

Before you begin

  • Use the student account supplied by your instructor, during your assigned time slot.
  • Each question has a time limit set by your instructor. Submitted answers cannot be changed.
  • Stay on the quiz page. Switching tabs or windows, refreshing, or leaving the page locks your attempt.
  • Use a stable internet connection. If an attempt locks because of a technical problem, contact your instructor.

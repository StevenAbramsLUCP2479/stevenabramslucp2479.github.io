These workshops are aimed at secondary-school mathematics teachers and focus on statistical reasoning, data analysis and the use of computational tools in statistics education.

Introduction to R programming

An introductory workshop on the use of R for statistical analysis and teaching. The workshop introduces the basic principles of working with data, performing statistical analyses and visualising results in R.

Organised in 2023 and 2024.

Statistical hypothesis testing

A workshop on the principles of statistical hypothesis testing, with attention to interpretation, common misconceptions and the connection between statistical theory and classroom applications.

Organised in 2024 and 2025.

Confidence intervals

A workshop on the construction and interpretation of confidence intervals and their role in statistical inference.

Organised in 2026.

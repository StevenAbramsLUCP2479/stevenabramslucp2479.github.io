I am **Steven Abrams**, a biostatistician and medical statistician with research interests in **infectious disease epidemiology, survival analysis, serology, mathematical and statistical modelling, and vaccine research**.

At **Hasselt University** and the **University of Antwerp**, my work focuses on the development and application of statistical and mathematical methods to better understand infectious disease dynamics, immunity, vaccination, and public health decision-making.

## Research

My research combines methodological developments in statistics with applications in infectious disease epidemiology and public health. Current and recent work includes:

- modelling antibody and serological data;
- infectious disease transmission and outbreak risk;
- survival and event-history analysis;
- individual heterogeneity and frailty modelling;
- vaccine effectiveness and correlates of protection;
- quantitative methods for public health decision-making.
